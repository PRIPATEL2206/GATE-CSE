# 🔁 Revision Strategy — GATE CS

> The exam isn't won by what you learn. It's won by what you **retain at high speed**.

---

## The 4-Layer Revision System

### Layer 1 — Same-Day (15 min, end of day)
- Re-read **Quick Revision** (Section 9) of today's topic.
- Re-state every formula from memory.

### Layer 2 — Spaced Touch (Day 3, Day 7)
- Skim **Short Notes** (Section 3) only.
- Redo 5 random PYQs of the topic.

### Layer 3 — Subject-End Sweep
- After all topics of a subject:
  - Read every *Quick Revision* + *Mistakes* section back-to-back (1 sitting).
  - Take **Subject Test** (`_tests/topic_tests/`).

### Layer 4 — Final Rounds (last 6 weeks)
- **Round 1 (3 weeks):** All 11 subjects' Quick Revision + PYQs only.
- **Round 2 (2 weeks):** Mistakes log + Pattern Recognition + tough PYQs.
- **Final week:** *Only* PYQs + 1 FLT every other day.

---

## What to Skip in Revision (deliberately)

- Long derivations — keep only the result + when-to-apply.
- Code/syntax in PDS — focus on tracing & complexity.
- Compiler internals beyond what PYQs ask.

## What to Never Skip

- Discrete Math identities & graph counts.
- DLD K-map and FF state tables.
- COA cache hit ratio + pipeline stalls computations.
- TOC closure properties table.
- OS scheduling / page replacement traces.
- DBMS canonical cover & lossless decomposition.
- CN subnetting, sliding window, TCP CC formulas.

---

## Active Recall Rituals

1. **Blank-Page Test** — close everything, write the topic from scratch in 7 min.
2. **Teach-Back** — explain the topic to an imaginary junior in 3 min.
3. **PYQ Speed Run** — 10 PYQs in 25 min with a timer.
4. **Formula-Card Drill** — flip 20 formulas/day during commutes.

---

## When You Forget Something

- Don't re-read the long notes. Go straight to **Mistakes log** + **Pattern Recognition** entry. If still blank, *then* read Short Notes.
- Schedule that topic for an extra Layer 2 touch the next day.

---

## Mistakes Log Discipline

Every wrong PYQ / practice question gets one row in [_progress/mistakes_log.md](../_progress/mistakes_log.md):

| Date | Topic | Question (1 line) | What I did wrong | Correct insight | Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Re-read this file **every Sunday**. If a row repeats — pull that topic into next week's plan.
