# 📅 Weekly Study Plan — GATE CS

> Beginner → Advanced progression. ~6 days study + 1 revision/test day.
>
> Adjust hours based on whether you have **6 months / 4 months / 2 months** to GATE — the *ratio* stays fixed.

---

## ⏱ Daily Time Block (target: 7 hrs/day)

| Block | Time | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| A | 2 hrs | New topic — Concept + Important Points + Short Notes (Sections 1–3) |
| B | 1 hr | Formulas/Tricks + worked examples (Section 4) |
| C | 1 hr | PYQs of the topic (Section 5) — solve, mark, review |
| D | 1.5 hrs | Practice Questions (Section 6) — 20+ |
| E | 30 min | Mistakes Log + Pattern Recognition (Sections 7–8) |
| F | 30 min | Quick Revision card (Section 9) — yesterday + today |
| G | 30 min | Aptitude (rotate quant / verbal / logical) |

**Working students:** compress to 3.5 hrs (A, C, F) + weekend deep work.

## ⚙️ Compressed Plans

### 4-Month Plan
- Weeks 1–14 → batches 1–26 (cover ~2 batches/week).
- Weeks 15–17 → 2 revision rounds + 6 FLTs.
- Drop low-yield depth in Compilers/COA history; keep PYQ + 9-section Quick Revision.

### 2-Month Sprint (revision-mode)
- Skip new theory you've never seen.
- Daily: 1 subject's *Quick Revision* + *PYQs* + *Mistakes* sections.
- Weeks 7–8 → only PYQs + 4 FLTs.

---

## 🔁 Daily Revision Loop

- **Yesterday's topic** Quick Revision card (15 min) — non-negotiable.
- **End-of-week:** redo all PYQs solved that week without notes.
- **End-of-month:** take the relevant Subject Test.

---

## 🧠 Habits That Compound

- Maintain a single **Mistakes Log** ([_progress/mistakes_log.md](../_progress/mistakes_log.md)) — every wrong answer goes here with *why*.
- Maintain a **Patterns** file per subject — recurring trick types from PYQs.
- Track time per question on PYQs; flag any > 4 min.
