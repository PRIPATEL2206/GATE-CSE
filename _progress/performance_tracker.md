## Trends to Watch

- **% trend over FLTs** — should be monotonically increasing.
- **Weakest subject across last 3 tests** — that's next week's focus.
- **Aptitude % < 80%** — emergency, fix in 3 days.
- **Time used > 175 min on FLT** — speed problem, not concept problem.

## Weekly Self-Review (every Sunday, 30 min)

1. Look at the last 7 days of entries.
2. Pick the *one* topic that hurt most.
3. Schedule it for **Layer 2 + 3 revision** (see [revision_strategy.md](../_plans/revision_strategy.md)) next week.
