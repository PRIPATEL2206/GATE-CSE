> Subject: General Aptitude — Quantitative
> GATE weight: **2 marks** every year. Tables, bar/line/pie charts, % comparisons.

## 1. Concept Explanation

### 1.1 Data Interpretation (DI)

Reading data presented in graphs / tables and computing answers.

**Common formats:**
- Tables.
- Bar charts (vertical / horizontal / stacked).
- Line graphs.
- Pie charts.
- Pictograms.
- Mixed data sets.

### 1.2 General Strategy

1. **Read the question** before scanning the data.
2. **Identify** which row/column/segment is needed.
3. **Compute** carefully; double-check units.
4. **Estimate** before computing exactly.

### 1.3 Tables

Plain rows × columns of numerical data. Direct lookup + comparison.

**Common operations:**
- Sum of row/column.
- Average across.
- Percentage of total.
- Compare two values.

### 1.4 Bar Charts

Vertical or horizontal bars; height = value.

**Stacked bars** show composition.
**Grouped bars** compare two or more series.

### 1.5 Line Graphs

Show trend over time/category.

**Slope** indicates rate of change.

### 1.6 Pie Charts

Each slice = % of whole.

**Convert:** total degrees = 360°. Each slice = (% × 360°/100) degrees.

### 1.7 Common DI Calculations

| Calculation | Tip |
|---|---|
| % of total | (value/total) × 100 |
| % change | (new − old)/old × 100 |
| Average | sum/count |
| Ratio | a:b |
| Compounded | year-over-year multiplier |
| Difference (absolute / percent) | (max − min) or % |

### 1.8 Speed Tips

- **Use approximations** (round to nearest 10).
- **Estimate first**, then refine.
- **Skip exact calculations** when ratio comparisons suffice.
- For "which is greater" questions, **avoid full math**.

### 1.9 Common GATE Patterns

**Pattern 1: Percentages from pie chart.**
"Given pie chart of expenses; what's value of category X if total = Rs Y?"

**Pattern 2: Trend from line graph.**
"Year-over-year growth rate for some metric."

**Pattern 3: Comparison from bar chart.**
"In which year did X exceed Y?"

**Pattern 4: Composite from table.**
"Average of column A for rows where column B > threshold."

### 1.10 Reading Pitfalls

- **Logarithmic scale:** check axis carefully.
- **Different scales** on dual-axis charts.
- **Percentages of percentages:** trap.
- **Total adding to 100% in pie chart:** always check.

### 1.11 Graphical Patterns

| Visual | Implies |
|---|---|
| Steep upward slope | Rapid increase |
| Plateau | No change |
| Spike | Outlier |
| Steady positive slope | Constant growth |

### 1.12 Complex DI Examples

#### Example: Pie Chart of Expenses

Total budget = Rs 60,000.
- Food 30% → 18,000.
- Rent 25% → 15,000.
- Travel 15% → 9,000.
- Education 20% → 12,000.
- Entertainment 10% → 6,000.

#### Example: Bar Chart Comparison

Year 1: A=100, B=80.
Year 2: A=120, B=110.
% increase: A 20%; B 37.5%. → B grew faster.

#### Example: Line Graph CAGR

CAGR = (end/start)^(1/n) − 1 where n = years.

### 1.13 Common GATE Questions

- Find missing value from totals.
- Compare two categories' growth.
- Compute % change between two years.
- Identify maximum/minimum in chart.
- Compute average across a window.

### 1.14 Approximation Examples

**% of large number:**
- 23% of 470 ≈ 23·5 = 115 (too rough); better: (1/4 − 0.02) · 470 ≈ 117.5 − 9.4 = 108.

**Quick division:**
- 400/7 ≈ 57.

### 1.15 Estimating Pie Slices

By eye:
- ~25% = quarter.
- ~33% = third.
- ~50% = half.

### 1.16 Multi-step DI

Sometimes requires combining multiple charts.
- Read all charts before solving.
- Watch units.

> **Summary:** DI rewards speed + accuracy. Read data carefully. Use approximations. Compute % change, ratios, averages cleanly. Always verify against original data.

## 2. Important Points

- **Pie chart total** = 100% = 360°.
- **% change formula:** (new − old)/old × 100.
- **CAGR** = (end/start)^(1/n) − 1.
- **Trend graphs:** slope = rate of change.
- **Bar charts:** compare easily by height.
- **Approximate first**, refine later.
- **Always check axis scales** (linear / logarithmic).

## 5. PYQs (with solutions)

### Q1. (GATE Aptitude 2017)
Pie chart shows 25% for X. If total = 200, X = ?
**Solution.** 50.

### Q2. (GATE Aptitude 2014)
Bar chart: A grew from 50 to 70. % increase?
**Solution.** 40%.

### Q3. (GATE Aptitude 2018)
Line graph: 2010 = 100, 2015 = 200. CAGR?
**Solution.** 2^(1/5) − 1 ≈ 14.87%.

### Q4. (GATE Aptitude 2008)
Table average of 5 values: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50.
**Solution.** 30.

### Q5. (GATE Aptitude 2010)
Pie chart: Food 30%, Rent 25%, Total = 60000. Food − Rent =?
**Solution.** (30−25)% × 60000 = 3000.

### Q6. (GATE Aptitude 2015)
Bar chart compare A and B over 3 years. A: 100,150,200; B: 80,160,180. Year B exceeded A?
**Solution.** Year 2 (160 > 150).

### Q7. (GATE Aptitude 2013)
Find % change from 80 to 120:
**Solution.** 50%.

### Q8. (GATE Aptitude 2007)
Pie chart slice 90°. % of total?
**Solution.** 25%.

### Q9. (GATE Aptitude 2003)
Average of bar chart values 5, 10, 15, 20.
**Solution.** 12.5.

### Q10. (GATE Aptitude 2009)
% growth A: 200 → 240; B: 100 → 130. Which higher?
**Solution.** A: 20%; B: 30%. B higher.

### Q11. (GATE Aptitude 2019)
CAGR from 50 to 100 in 4 years:
**Solution.** 2^(1/4) − 1 ≈ 18.92%.

### Q12. (GATE Aptitude 2020)
Pie chart category X: 72°. Total = 1000. X = ?
**Solution.** (72/360) × 1000 = 200.

### Q13. (GATE Aptitude 2021)
Find minimum value in given data set.
**Solution.** Direct lookup.

### Q14. (GATE Aptitude 2016)
% of total spent on X if X = 30 out of 200.
**Solution.** 15%.

### Q15. (GATE Aptitude 2011)
Annual growth rate for 100 → 121 → 146.41:
**Solution.** 21% → 21% → CAGR = 21%.

## 7. Mistakes

| # | Trap | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Misread pie chart percentages | Verify total = 100%. |
| 2 | Confuse % change vs ratio | Different. |
| 3 | Forget axis units | Always check labels. |
| 4 | Compute with original instead of new | (new−old)/old. |
| 5 | Approximate too aggressively | Refine if close. |
| 6 | Assume linear scale | Check log axis. |
| 7 | Bar height ≠ true value | Read carefully. |
| 8 | Over-compute when comparing | Ratios suffice. |
| 9 | Skip context | Read question. |
| 10 | Ignore footnotes/legend | Critical info. |
