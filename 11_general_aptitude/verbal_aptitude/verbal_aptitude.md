## 1. Concept Explanation

### 1.1 Parts of Speech

| Part | Function | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Noun | Person, place, thing | book, Alice |
| Pronoun | Replaces noun | he, she, it |
| Verb | Action / state | run, is |
| Adjective | Modifies noun | quick |
| Adverb | Modifies verb / adj | quickly |
| Preposition | Relates noun to other | on, at, in |
| Conjunction | Connects | and, but, because |
| Interjection | Emotion | wow! |

### 1.2 Tenses

| Tense | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Simple present | base form | I write |
| Simple past | -ed (regular) | I wrote |
| Simple future | will + base | I will write |
| Present continuous | am/is/are + -ing | I am writing |
| Present perfect | have/has + past participle | I have written |
| Past perfect | had + past participle | I had written |
| Future perfect | will have + past participle | I will have written |
| Present perfect continuous | have/has been + -ing | I have been writing |

### 1.3 Subject-Verb Agreement

- **Singular subject** → singular verb.
- **Plural subject** → plural verb.
- **Either/Neither/Each/Every** → singular.
- **Either... or / Neither... nor:** verb agrees with **nearer** subject.
- **None** can be both depending on meaning.
- **Collective nouns** can be singular or plural.

### 1.4 Articles

| Article | Use |
|---|---|
| a | Before consonant sound (a book, a university) |
| an | Before vowel sound (an apple, an hour) |
| the | Specific reference |

**No article** with most proper nouns, abstract nouns (general).

### 1.5 Prepositions

Common confusions:
- **In / On / At:** "in the room" (enclosed), "on the table" (surface), "at the door" (point).
- **Since / For:** "since 2010" (point), "for 5 years" (duration).
- **Among / Between:** "between two", "among many".
- **Beside / Besides:** "beside" = next to; "besides" = in addition to.

### 1.6 Active vs Passive Voice

- **Active:** subject does the action. "She writes a book."
- **Passive:** subject receives. "A book is written by her."

**Form:** be + past participle.

### 1.7 Direct vs Indirect Speech

- Direct: "He said, 'I am happy.'"
- Indirect: "He said that he was happy."

**Tense shift:** present → past; past → past perfect; will → would.

### 1.8 Reported Speech Rules

| Direct | Indirect |
|---|---|
| Today | That day |
| Tomorrow | Next day |
| Yesterday | Previous day |
| Now | Then |
| Here | There |
| This | That |
| These | Those |

### 1.9 Common Grammar Errors

- **Misplaced modifier.**
- **Dangling participle.**
- **Subject-verb mismatch.**
- **Pronoun antecedent unclear.**
- **Run-on sentence.**
- **Comma splice.**
- **Parallelism.**

### 1.14 Reading Comprehension Tips

1. **Skim first** to grasp main idea.
2. **Read questions** before detailed reading.
3. **Identify keywords** in passage.
4. **Eliminate clearly wrong** options.
5. **Direct evidence** preferred over inference.

### 1.15 Question Types in RC

| Type | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Main idea | First/last paragraph |
| Detail | Locate keyword |
| Inference | Reason from text |
| Tone | Adjective / mood |
| Vocabulary in context | Use surrounding sentences |
| Author's purpose | Why was this written? |

### 1.16 Common Confused Words

- **Affect / Effect:** affect (verb, influence); effect (noun, result).
- **Their / There / They're:** possession / location / contraction.
- **Its / It's:** possessive / "it is".
- **Your / You're:** possessive / "you are".
- **Then / Than:** time / comparison.
- **Lay / Lie:** transitive (lay something) / intransitive.
- **Principal / Principle:** main / belief.
- **Stationary / Stationery:** still / paper goods.

### 1.17 Word Pairs (Analogies)

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Synonym | brave : courageous |
| Antonym | hot : cold |
| Cause-effect | study : knowledge |
| Part-whole | wheel : car |
| Tool-action | pen : write |
| Worker-tool | doctor : stethoscope |
| Item-category | rose : flower |

### 1.18 Sentence Completion

Choose word(s) that fit context grammatically and semantically.

Strategy: identify clue words (although, however, similarly).

### 1.19 Para Jumbles

Order sentences to form coherent paragraph.

**Tips:**
- Find topic sentence.
- Locate transitions (however, therefore).
- Pronoun antecedents.

### 1.20 Common GATE Patterns

- Sentence correction.
- Vocabulary (synonym/antonym).
- Reading comprehension (1 passage, 2 questions).
- Idioms.
- Para completion / jumbles.

> **Summary:** Master grammar (tenses, agreement, articles, prepositions), vocabulary (roots/prefixes), idioms, and RC strategies. Direct evidence beats inference in RC.

## 2. Important Points

- **Subject-verb agreement** with either/neither uses nearer subject.
- **Articles** depend on sound (a, an).
- **Active ↔ passive:** subject becomes object.
- **Tense backshift** in indirect speech.
- **Misplaced modifier** changes meaning.
- **Vocabulary** via roots and prefixes.
- **Idiom = non-literal meaning.**
- **RC main idea** in first / last paragraph.
- **Eliminate** wrong options first.
- **Affect / effect** common confusion.

## 5. PYQs (with solutions)

### Q1. (GATE Verbal 2017)
"Either Tom or his sisters _____ at home." Fill blank.
**Solution.** "are" — verb agrees with nearer subject (sisters).

### Q2. (GATE Verbal 2014)
Synonym of "lethargic":
**Solution.** Sluggish.

### Q3. (GATE Verbal 2018)
Active to passive: "She wrote a book."
**Solution.** "A book was written by her."

### Q4. (GATE Verbal 2008)
Antonym of "obscure":
**Solution.** Clear / obvious.

### Q5. (GATE Verbal 2010)
Idiom "burn the midnight oil":
**Solution.** Work late.

### Q6. (GATE Verbal 2015)
"He has been waiting _____ 2 hours." Use **for** or **since**?
**Solution.** "for" (duration).

### Q7. (GATE Verbal 2013)
Affect vs effect:
**Solution.** Affect = influence (verb); effect = result (noun).

### Q8. (GATE Verbal 2007)
Sentence: "The team has decided to ____ the project." Fill: "begin" or "began"?
**Solution.** "begin" (after to).

### Q9. (GATE Verbal 2003)
"Neither Alice nor her friends ____ aware." Fill blank.
**Solution.** "are" (nearer subject plural).

### Q10. (GATE Verbal 2009)
RC: identify main idea.
**Solution.** Look at topic sentences.

### Q11. (GATE Verbal 2019)
Synonym of "ephemeral":
**Solution.** Short-lived.

### Q12. (GATE Verbal 2020)
Their / there / they're: "_____ going to the park."
**Solution.** "They're" (= they are).

### Q13. (GATE Verbal 2021)
Choose word: "His argument was _____ but unconvincing."
**Solution.** "logical" / "well-reasoned" (clue: but unconvincing).

### Q14. (GATE Verbal 2016)
Para jumble: identify first sentence.
**Solution.** Most general topic sentence.

### Q15. (GATE Verbal 2011)
Indirect speech: He said, "I will go tomorrow."
**Solution.** He said that he would go the next day.

## 7. Mistakes

| # | Trap | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Affect/effect swapped | Verb vs noun. |
| 2 | Misplaced modifier | Place near modified word. |
| 3 | SVA with intervening phrase | Identify true subject. |
| 4 | Article based on letter | Use sound. |
| 5 | Tense not shifted in indirect | Backshift required. |
| 6 | Idiom taken literally | Look for meaning. |
| 7 | RC inference without evidence | Direct evidence preferred. |
| 8 | Synonym = exact match | Approximate is enough. |
| 9 | Comma splice | Use semicolon or conjunction. |
| 10 | Pronoun without clear antecedent | Restructure. |
